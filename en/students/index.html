About Us

Who do we support?

Czech and Slovak students who have received an official offer to an undergraduate or graduate course (not a DPhil) at the University of Oxford or the University of Cambridge and who require financial assistance.

​

Eligibility Criteria

  • Czech or Slovak citizenship or long-term residency

  • Official offer letter from Oxford or Cambridge (Bachelor or Master only)

  • Strong academic record and a clear motivation for your chosen course

How It Works

Our organisation serves as an intermediary between students and donors. We do not choose recipients, guarantee funding, or define the conditions of support ourselves. Instead, donors decide independently which students they wish to support and under what terms.

01

You submit your application

Fill out the application. Your profile will then be reviewed, and we may contact you with suggestions for improvements to make your profile more attractive to sponsors.

03

Donors recommend whom to support

They may contact you directly or through us. 

05​

Tuition is paid to the university

We mediate of the payment of the fees and ensure the support is transferred correctly.

02

Your profile is shared with potential donors

Your profile will be published on this website and sent out directly to our network of donors. The date of this release will be depending on when you submit your application.

04

You and the donor agree on the form of support

We will help establish a contractual framework for the financial support, although some donors may require extra conditions. If a donor proposes unclear, unexpected, or uncomfortable terms, you should reach out to us immediately.

Sponsorship Conditions and Student Expectations

Because the support comes directly from donors, conditions of may vary. However, if the donor decides to donate via our foundation, these funds will be available only to cover tuition fees paid directly to the university.

 

To ensure smooth and effective fundraising, students are expected to:

  • Provide complete and accurate information in the application and all following communication

  • Communicate promptly with donors (or through us)

  • Submit their tuition invoice and other required documents on time so we can process the payment

  • Respect the terms agreed upon in their donor contract

If a donor suggests unreasonable or unclear conditions, contact us immediately and we will help mediate and ensure the arrangement remains appropriate.

FAQs

What documents do I need to apply?
A full checklist is listed above.

​​

How are students selected?

We only connect admitted students to donors; the donors recommend/designate who will be supported.

​

Can I apply without an offer letter?
No. You must wait until you receive an official offer from Oxford or Cambridge.

​

Can multiple donors support me?
Yes. Many students receive partial contributions from several donors.

​

What if I only get enough funding for a part of my studies?
Once your get enough support to complete a full year, we recommend that you enroll on your course and use our platform (and other means) to fundraise for subsequent year(s) next.

​

Is funding only for tuition?

Under our recommended support framework, ​only tuition fees are funded. You can however arrange different conditions with your donor.

​

Can I ask for help if a donor proposes strange or uncomfortable conditions?
Absolutely. Contact us immediately and we will help mediate.
