What we collect

The sign-up form on the home page, the donors page and the scholarship seekers page collects your first name, last name and e-mail address. Filling it in is voluntary and lets us get back to you about the project and ways to support a student.

Every page view processes your IP address and basic browser information. Without it the page could not be delivered at all; this happens on every website.

Analytics

We use Umami Cloud. It measures without cookies and without profiling individuals, which is why you will not see a cookie banner here. We care about which pages people read and where they came from, not who you are. Processing region: [DOPLNIT: EU or US].

Language choice on your first visit

On your first visit we look up your country from your IP address through ipwho.is so we can switch you to Czech, Slovak or English. Your IP address reaches that provider as part of the lookup. Your language is then stored in your browser and the lookup is not repeated.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct or erase it, restrict processing, port it elsewhere, and object to processing. You can withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time — write to stipendium@oxbridgestipendium.org and we will sort it out.

If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can contact the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection or the Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection.

Minors

The project is for students holding an offer from Oxford or Cambridge, so normally people over 18. We do not deliberately collect children's data.
